Who provides HEditor

HEditor 绘影 (HEditor) is provided by GBTechReviews. Contact the developer about privacy or support at huaweiproreview@gmail.com. This policy covers the HarmonyOS NEXT app; external websites have their own policies.

Your selected photos stay on your device

HEditor processes the images you choose, their embedded metadata, edit settings, masks, text and collages on your device to provide editing, draft recovery and export. Private source copies, rendered copies and edit history are saved in the app's private storage. Photos and EXIF metadata can contain personal information, including faces or location recorded by your camera. HEditor does not upload this material to the developer or use it to identify you.

No in-app tracking or cloud editing

The app has no registration, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking identifiers or automatic developer crash-report uploads. It does not request internet access. Cloud AI editing is not available. Optional subject selection and removal use device processing and HarmonyOS system capabilities when you request them. System services, app distribution and operating-system diagnostics are managed separately by Huawei under their policies.

Permissions, exports and clipboard

You choose photos through the system Photo Picker. The app does not request blanket access to your library, camera, microphone, contacts or location. A system SaveButton authorizes each Gallery export. Sharing opens the system share sheet and sends only what you choose to the destination you select. Haptics use the vibration permission and can be switched off in Settings. A Copy action writes the chosen text to the clipboard; HEditor does not read your clipboard in the background.

System capabilities and third-party sharing

HarmonyOS Photo Picker gives access to the photos you select. Image Kit reads and renders pixels and metadata locally. Core Vision subject segmentation processes the selected image on the device only when requested; HEditor does not perform identity recognition. These are Huawei platform capabilities, not a bundled analytics or advertising SDK. No photo information is automatically shared with a third-party SDK by HEditor. A system sharing destination receives the exported file only when you select it. Huawei privacy information: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/ .

Local settings and Pro activation

Theme, haptics, export preferences and Pro ownership are stored locally. A receipt code is checked on your device using SHA-256; the app stores the resulting ownership flag, not a payment-card number. The app does not send the code to a server. There is no HEditor account or online purchase-verification service.

Retention, backups and deletion

Drafts, source copies and settings remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Unreferenced working copies may remain until that deletion. System backup or device migration may include app data if enabled in your device settings. You can manage those backups through the operating system. Clearing app storage removes local drafts, settings and Pro ownership; retain your receipt code to reactivate. Gallery exports, shared copies and existing system backups must be deleted separately in their respective apps or services. The developer cannot remotely access or erase your local photos.

When you contact us or make an external purchase

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you send. We use that information to answer your request and resolve problems, based on our legitimate interest in providing support or to perform the requested service. If you buy Pro through a separate Ko-fi listing when available, Ko-fi and its payment provider handle checkout. As seller we may receive transaction details such as the purchaser's contact details, amount and payment reference, to deliver the purchase and meet legal obligations. We do not receive your full card details through HEditor. Support and transaction records are retained only as needed for the request, disputes and applicable legal obligations. They are not used for advertising.

External pages and international processing

Opening the public policy pages uses your browser. GitHub hosts those pages and may process web request information under its own privacy statement. Ko-fi, payment providers and your email provider may process information outside your country under their own terms. Opening an external page or sending a message is your choice; it does not upload your photo collection from HEditor. Avoid sending sensitive photos or unnecessary personal information in support requests.

Your choices and rights

You may refuse photo processing on the welcome screen, or withdraw it in Settings > Withdraw photo consent. Photo tools then remain inactive until you explicitly agree again. Existing drafts are retained until you clear app storage; Gallery exports and backups are separate. You may stop processing by closing the app, choose different photos, turn haptics off and manage local data or backups as described above. For personal information you send to the developer, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority; UK users may contact the ICO at ico.org.uk. There is no account-deletion process because the app has no account. We respond to privacy requests within 15 working days, or sooner where applicable law requires.

Children and policy changes

The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. Parents or guardians should supervise external purchases and support messages from children. We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. A new data-transfer feature would require an updated notice and any necessary permission before use.

提供者与联系方式

HEditor 绘影（HEditor）由 GBTechReviews 提供。隐私或支持问题请联系 huaweiproreview@gmail.com。本政策适用于 HarmonyOS NEXT 应用；外部网站适用各自的隐私政策。

所选照片在设备上处理

HEditor 在您的设备上处理您主动选择的图像、图像内嵌的元数据、编辑参数、蒙版、文字和拼图，以提供编辑、草稿恢复及导出。原图副本、渲染副本和编辑历史保存在应用私有存储中。照片及 EXIF 元数据可能包含人像或相机记录的位置等个人信息。HEditor 不会将这些内容上传给开发者，也不会用于识别您的身份。

无应用内跟踪或云端编辑

本应用无需注册，不含广告、分析 SDK 或跟踪标识符，也不会自动向开发者上传崩溃报告。应用不申请联网权限，不提供云端 AI 编辑。您主动使用的主体选择和移除功能通过设备处理及 HarmonyOS 系统能力实现。系统服务、应用分发和操作系统诊断由华为依据其政策另行管理。

权限、导出与剪贴板

您通过系统照片选择器主动选择照片。应用不申请整库相册、相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位权限。每次保存至图库时由系统保存按钮授权。分享操作打开系统分享面板，仅将您选择的内容交给您指定的目标。触觉反馈使用振动权限，可在设置中关闭。复制操作仅将所选文字写入剪贴板；HEditor 不会在后台读取剪贴板。

系统能力与第三方共享

HarmonyOS 照片选择器提供对所选照片的访问；Image Kit 在本地读取和渲染像素及元数据；Core Vision 主体分割仅在您主动请求时，在设备上处理所选图像，HEditor 不进行身份识别。这些是华为平台能力，不是内置广告或分析 SDK。HEditor 不会自动向第三方 SDK 共享照片信息。仅在您指定分享目标时，系统才将导出文件交给该目标。华为隐私信息：https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/ 。

本地设置与 Pro 激活

主题、触觉反馈、导出偏好及 Pro 授权状态保存在本地。收据激活码通过设备上的 SHA-256 校验；应用保存授权标记，不保存银行卡号，也不将激活码发送至服务器。HEditor 不设用户账户或在线交易验证服务。

保存期限、备份与删除

草稿、原图副本和设置会保留，直至您清除应用存储或卸载应用。未引用的工作副本也可能保留至删除时。如设备设置启用了系统备份或设备迁移，应用数据可能包含在其中；请通过操作系统管理这些备份。清除应用存储会删除本地草稿、设置及 Pro 授权标记；请保留收据激活码以便重新激活。图库导出、已分享的副本及已有系统备份需分别在对应应用或服务中删除。开发者无法远程访问或删除您的本地照片。

您主动联系或在外部购买时

如您主动发送邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址及您发送的信息或附件。我们为答复请求、解决问题和提供您要求的服务而使用这些信息，适用履行服务所需或提供支持的合法利益等依据。如将来您通过独立的 Ko-fi 商品页面购买 Pro，结账由 Ko-fi 及其支付服务商处理。作为卖方，我们可能收到购买者联系方式、金额及交易编号等必要信息，以交付商品并履行法律义务。HEditor 不会向我们提供完整银行卡信息。支持和交易记录仅在处理请求、争议及适用法律义务所需期间保留，不用于广告。

外部页面与跨境处理

在线政策页面由您的浏览器打开，并由 GitHub 托管；GitHub 可能依据其隐私声明处理网页请求信息。Ko-fi、支付服务商及邮件服务商可能依据各自的条款在您所在国家或地区以外处理信息。是否打开外部页面或发送消息由您选择，这不会使 HEditor 上传您的照片库。支持请求中请避免发送敏感照片或不必要的个人信息。

您的选择与权利

您可在首次使用页面拒绝照片处理，或在设置的 Withdraw photo consent（撤回照片处理同意）中撤回。同意撤回后，照片工具保持停用，直至您再次明确同意。已有草稿会保留至您清除应用存储；图库导出和备份需另行处理。您可关闭应用以停止处理、另选照片、关闭触觉反馈，并按上述方式管理本地数据或备份。对于您主动提供给开发者的个人信息，可联系开发者依法请求查阅、更正、删除、限制、反对处理或获取可携带副本。您有权反对基于合法利益的处理，也可向所在地的数据保护监管机构投诉；英国用户可联系 ico.org.uk。应用没有账户，因此无需办理账户注销。我们会在15个工作日内处理隐私请求；如适用法律要求更短期限，则遵守该期限。
